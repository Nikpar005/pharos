# Code of Conduct

Pharos follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the people who will use this app in an emergency. Harassment, insults and discrimination of any kind are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer at **nikpar005@gmail.com**. All reports are handled confidentially.
